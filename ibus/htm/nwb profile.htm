新 巴 ─ 一切從新開始
NWLB - We must stop meting like this

一九九八年八月三十一日晚上至九月一日清晨的數小時內，不少市民，包括政府官員、外國遊客及巴士迷哥迷姐等，從北角跑到柴灣，又從柴灣跑到小西灣，接著又冒雨趕往中環港澳碼頭巴士總站，然後又折回柴灣巴士廠。

在這數小時內，各人都見證了新世界第一巴士服務有限公司派出自己的車隊，從零時開始投入服務市民；同時亦目睹新巴接收前中華巴士公司旗下的七百一十輛各類型巴士，與及各廠房的順利交接。在短短的五個月內，新巴成功取得政府批出的巴士專營權，到購入新巴士，部署全盤接收路線網絡，重新聘用前中巴的員工，直至全面運作，這一連串的改變，本港市民看在眼裡，無不嘖嘖稱奇。還有，新巴投入服務的首個月內，它的車隊變化同樣地十分迅速，除新增購的巴士以新色彩示人外，來自中巴車隊的空調巴士，亦陸續以新巴色彩投入服務。

In the last hour of 31 August 1998, a number of citizens gathered with others including government officials, tourists and bus enthusiasts in chasing from North Point, Chai Wan and then Siu Sai Wan. Later on, everyone took to the Bus Terminus outside Macau Terminal in Central under pouring rain and back to Chai Wan Bus Depot.

In these few hours, everyone witnessed for the commencement of the bus fleet of New World First Bus into active service from midnight. Everyone also witnessed the smooth handover of CMB's 710 different bus models to the new company.

Within the short period of 5 months, NWLB successfully acquired the franchise from the government, went buying new buses , planned the whole bus network and re-employed former staff from CMB. All these went before our eyes and earned admiration. In the first month since its operation, there have also been repid changes to the NWLB fleet. In addition to buying more and more new buses, all air-conditioned buses bought from CMB will be facelifted with the distinctive colours of the new company.
