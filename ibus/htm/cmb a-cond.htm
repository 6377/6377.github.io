名稱：「丹尼士」禿鷹型十一米(空調)
Name : Dennis Condor 11 Metre (Air-conditioned)

車隊編號：DA
Fleet No: DA

內容簡介：

在九巴試驗「巨龍」期間，中巴則一擲千金，並沒有經過試驗便一口氣購入十二部十一米空調版「禿鷹」，並於九零年七月至八月期間匆匆投入服務，為被入侵的巴士網絡進行首度反擊。反擊的第一炮，是開辦首條空調巴士線537路線，以對抗非專利巴士37R路線。但計劃中的第二炮卻觸礁，中巴申辦590路線以對抗90R路線被拒，之後便改為開辦504路線。

車輛動力是來自中巴首次接觸的「康明斯」L10引擎，並同時帶動由「日本電裝」供應的空調系統，再配合「福伊特」DIWA863三前速全自動波箱，便有了空調巴士的基本組合。座位是中巴標準的一整套座墊，特別的地方是上、落車門側的扶手桿是蘋果綠色的。

為開辦減輕彌敦道交通擠塞路線的300、301、302、303、305、307、336、337及348路線，與及增加南區543路線、23號路線加入空調巴士服務及擴展590、592及595路線的陣容，二十四輛及二十輛十一米空調版「禿鷹」新車，己分別於九一年五月至八月期間，及九二年八月至十月期間加入中巴車隊，使其「鷹抓」擴展至青衣、大埔等地。

全球最後的十輛配用改良版「日本電裝」空調系統的「禿鷹」亦已經在九六年底投入服務，之後的「禿鷹」生產線則由新的代替品取代。

Description :

While KMB was still evaluating Air-conditioned 'Dragon' buses, CMB bought a total number of 12 11Metre Air-conditioned 'Condor' buses in a row without evaluating. These 12 'Condor' buses were hastily commissioned into service from July to August in 1990 to counter-attack the intrusion of bus service of CMB by other bus competitors. The first one was the air-conditioned route 537 to compete for the Estate route 37R. But the plan to launch route 590 against Estate route 90R was halted and was then changed to route 504.

These buses have 'Cummins' LT10 engines, the first for CMB. With the addition of the air-conditioning unit from 'Nippon Denso' and the 3-speed full-automatic transmission from 'Voith' DIWA863, they formed the basic specifications for all future air-conditioned buses. The seating was CMB's standard one-piece seats. The special feature was the apple-green coloured handles at the doors.

CMB introduced 2 batches of 24 and 20 11Metre air-conditioned 'Condors' from May to August 1991 and from August to October 1993 recpectively. These were used to relieve the high demands for route 300, 302, 303, 305, 307, 336, 337 & 348 on Nathan Road as well as to add air-conditioning to routes 543 for Island South and route 23. The buses were also used to add the bulk of route 590, 592 & 595. The presence of 'Condors', therefore, were seen as far away as Tsing Yi Island and Tai Po.

Until the end of 1996, the last 10 'Condor' buses in the world to have 'Nippon Denso' air-conditioning units were commissioned. Thereafter, the production of 'Condors' ceased and replaced by other models.


中巴失去南區260路線，但仍可在某些假日營辦260X路線。這部『丹尼士』「巨龍」DA24 是在端午節從中區開往淺水灣，單程車費每位六元正。
Route 260X was operated by CMB on the occasion of certain holidays. "Dennis" 'Condor', DA24 was seen on Tuen Ng Festival Holiday on its way to Repulse Bay.


『丹尼士』「巨龍」DA54行走過海隧道路線301，是一條為減輕彌敦道擠塞而開辦的早晨特別路線，逢星期一至五提供服務，來往紅磡隧道口及上環。
DA54 served the Cross Harbour Tunnel Route 301 early in the morning from Monday to Saturday, travel between Hunghom Cross Harbour Tunnel and Sheung Wan.

DA1是中巴首部雙層空調巴士，這天在冷氣巴士線595服務。
DA1 was the first air-conditioned double decker bus in CMB fleet and was seen on air-conditioned route 595.

DA11披上全白色的車身，只能從車身上的中巴標誌去確認它的身份。
DA11 had a fully white colour livery and could be recognised by its CMB logo.

DA56在23號的路線上，輔助其他非空調巴士服務。
CMB route 23 was a combine service route by air-conditioned and non air-conditioned buses. DA56 was seen here in the early morning.

DA90行走A20路線，該路線連接舊機場──啟德機場及中環，正途經太古廣場。
DA90 served the route A20 between Kai Tak Airport and Central via Pacific Place.

DA16是中巴購入的第二批DA空調巴士，在1991年5月投入服務，加入空調線504行走，簇新的車身上面，有中巴空調的特別字樣。
DA16 was the second batch of DA air-conditioned bus that purchased by CMB, it started to serve on route 504 on May 1991, with special 'air-conditioned' words on her body.
DA43改用了統一化中巴空調巴士色彩，簡潔悅目，只是六隻車輪仍然沿用中巴傳統的忌廉色。
CMB changed the outlook of DA43 to standard colour model for air-conditioned buses, it looked distinct, clean and tidy, only the wheels remained CMB traditional model colour, creamy in colour.

DA型空調巴士的車尾，是「日本電裝」供應的空調機組合，位於引擎室的上方，空調機的壓縮器直接由引擎的曲軸驅動。
The air-conditioning plant by 'Nippon Denso' of Japan was neatly concealed in the space above the engine at the rear part of all DA air-conditioned buses.

1990年6月底，中巴首部DA雙層空調巴士 裝嵌完成後往運輸署驗車中心驗車。「都普」車身的全部車窗，均設有可上鎖的小窗。
The bodywork of the first air-conditioned bus of CMB was completed, and went to Transportation Department for examining at the end of June 1990. The CMB version of the 'Duple Metsec' body featured small UK-style window sliders which could be locked.
