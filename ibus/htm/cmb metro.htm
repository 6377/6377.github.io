名稱：「都城嘉慕」都城一型
Name : Metro Cammell Weymann Metrobus I

車隊編號：MC
Fleet No: MC

內容簡介：

為提供較優質的服務，中巴於七八年率先引進十二輛第三代後置引擎的「都城一型」巴士，行走豪華巴士路線260及262線。該十二輛「都城」巴士均採用「福伊特」DIWA851全自動傳動系統、全氣墊式懸掛系統、淺藍色車窗玻璃及反潮流的二加二的高背座位編排。而單一車門上落乘客，更方便的是，該兩線乘客落車時繳付車費的獨特分段收費安排。

中巴推出這兩條豪華巴士路線，原意是希望吸引南區的私家車車主，放棄使用座駕而改乘坐MC豪華型巴士。以七十年代的水平，MC除了缺乏今天的空調系統外，其實已具備一切豪華巴士的設備及配搭。

到九一年下半年，中巴將冷氣的『丹尼士』「飛驃」巴士投入260及262路線服務後，MC豪華巴士便功成身退。但脫離了260及262路線後，其單一車門設計便成了乘客上落的一種障礙，因此，不能編往乘客量較大、上落車次數頻密及交通繁忙的路線行走。後期，十二輛「都城」巴士經常行走13及63號路線。

Description :

In order to provide better service, CMB first introduced 12 third-generation rear-engine buses in 1978 - 'Metrobus' on Luxury route 260 & 262. These 12 'Metrobuses' had DTWA851 full-automatic transmission and all air-suspension system. They had light blue windows and the new 2+2 high-back seating. The method of paying fare was unique on these two routes when passengers could pay sectional fares and do so only on getting off the bus. A single door was therefore used for these purpose.

CMB introduced these two routes tried to attract private-car owners of Island South to use public transport instead of their own cars. Considering the standards of the late 70s when air-conditioning was still not available. 'Metrobuses' already had all the luxury items of the present day Luxury ones.

In the later half of 1991, CMB replaced the MCs with "Dennis" 'Dart' buses to serve on routes 260 & 262, the MC buses quitted these 2 luxury routes then. However, the single door design of MC buses became a problem for routes that require frequent loading with a large capacity of passengers. Later on , the only regular routes that served by the 'Metrobuses' were limited to No. 13 & 63.

名稱：「都城嘉慕」都城一型十一米
Name : Metro Cammell Weymann Metrobus I 11 Metre

車隊編號：MB
Fleet No: MB

內容簡介：

首輛而又被稱為「超級珍寶」的「都城一型」十一米巴士──MB1，於一九七八年投入服務，並喚起各大巴士生產商去鑽研製造更大型的雙層巴士的可行性。何謂「超級珍寶」？在當時，「珍寶」是被用作宣傳『利蘭』「Fleetline」的特大載客量。顧名思義，「超級珍寶」即是指其載客容量更大的巴士了。

簡單地形容一下「都城型」十一米，是「都城型」九點七米的加長及雙門版，其實際長度則是十一點四五米，機械結構及性能均與九點七米版相似，都是使用「福伊特」DIWA851三前速波箱及「吉拿」6LXB引擎等配搭，但車廂內的座位編排則完全是不一樣的。普通乳膠座墊的座椅，配合三加二的座位編排，下層扶手橫桿裝有大量皮製手環，使其准許載客量增達146名，後運輸署修例將載客量減至129名。另外，由於，「都城型」十一米是一款兩軸巴士，當時在過海隧時的費用只是十五元，但與九巴所選同廠後期的「都城型」十一米三軸巴士相比，隧道費已省了十元。

後來，市場上出現更大載客量的雙層巴士，所以「都城型」十一米在全球只生產四十輛，並全數提供給中巴。當時，該四十輛巴士分別服務於東區線及隧道線，計有720、104、105、114及641路線，也有用作101、102、111及112的後備車。

Description :

The first "Super-Jumbo" 'Metrobus I' 11 Metre was commissioned in 1978 and alerted the major bus manufacturers into designing even larger Double Deckers for practical use. The word "Jumbo" was then used ro depict the high carrying capacity of the "Leyland" 'Fleetline' and it followed that "Super-Jumbo" was then used to refer to an even higher capacity buses.

The "Metro Cammell" 11 Metre was essentially an elonggated double-door version of its 9.7 Metre counterpart. Its effective length was 11.45 Metre but it had basically the same mechanical specifications of the 9.7 Metre version. It used the same DIWA851 3-speed gearbox and 'Gardner' 6LXB engine but the seating arrangment was completely different. It had foam-cushioned seats arranged in rows of 3+2. The lower deck grab-rails had a large number of leather rings to increase its passenger capacity to 146. Later, it was reduced by the Transport Department to only 129. In addition, the 'Metrobus' 11 Metre had only 2-axles and costs only $15 to cross the Cross-Harbour Tunnel, effectively reducing the tunnel toll by $10 when compared with KMB's 3-axle 11.3 Metre 'Metrobuses'.

Later on, the market had more options available to bus companies to choose from, therefore only 40 'Metrobus' 11 Metre went into production worldwide. CMB had all of them and they were used in the Eastern District and Tunnel routes including routes 720, 104, 105, 114 & 641. Some were used as reserve for routes 101, 102, 111 & 112.

名稱：「都城嘉慕」都城二型十二米
Name : Metro Cammell Weymann Metrobus II 12 Metre

車隊編號：ML
Fleet No: ML

內容簡介：

一九八一年四月，在天星碼頭舉行有關『都城嘉慕』「超級都城二型」十二米巴士移交儀式中，屬於中巴的主角「ML1」，於同年的五月五日登記為CM8935，亦同時是全港首部登記的三軸巴士。當時新車的准許載客量為一百七十人，ML1與九巴3M1同樣擁有上車門及落車門各一，然而ML1則以企位為主，因此下層只有三十三個座位。ML2於同年十月二十三日登記，車牌號碼為CR4882，亦成為全港首部裝有三扇上落門的巴士，准許載客量為一百六十九人，初期ML1及ML2 同被派往行走112路線。「都城一型」及「都城二型」分別在於車頭的擋風玻璃，「都城一型」的擋風玻璃是高低不對稱的，有別於「都城二型」的高低對稱擋風玻璃。

在九巴尚在試驗多個生產商所提供的三軸巴士時，中巴已訂購四十二輛「超級都城型」，ML3至ML44於八三年二月至八四年間登記，以增強該公司在隧線巴士的運載能力。

為配合新市鎮新隧道及港島南區的人口增加，中巴後期再增訂購多四十輛「超級都城型」，ML45至ML84在八七年十月至八八年十一月間投入服務。全數八十四輛「超級都城型」均由「吉拿」6LXCT渦輪增壓引擎所發出的210bhp動力推動。並配合「福伊特」DIWA851傳動系統及全氣墊懸掛系統。在繁忙時間裡，「超級都城型」仍不失為中巴運載大量乘客的首選車。

初期「超級都城型」曾為90、92、96、97、99、107及182線服務，而後來則分別服務於8、42、101、104、195、106、111、112、116、690、780及788路線。

Description :

In April 1981, MCB took delivery of the ML1 in a ceremony held in front of Star Ferry Pier. This was a "Metro Cammell" 12M 'Super Metrobus II', the first 3-axle double-decker bus in Hong Kong and it was registered as CM8955 on May 5. The bus had a carrying capacity of 170. Like the 3M1 of KMB, ML1 had separate entry and exit doors. ML1 was designed to cater for more standing passengers and it had therefore only 33 seats on the lower deck. ML2 was registered on October 23 of the same year as CR4882 that was the first bus in Hong Kong with a total of 3 entry and exit doors, its permitted passenger capacity was 169. The windscreen of 'Super Metrobus II' was differed from 'Metrobus I'.

ML1 and ML2 were used on route 112 in the early days. While its counterpart on KMB was still experimenting with 3-axle buses, CMB already ordered 42 'Super Metrobuses'. ML3 to ML44 were then commissioned from February 1983 to January 1984 to increase CMB's Tunnel route capacity.

Later on, CMB also ordered an additional 40 'Super Metrobuses' for the New Town and New Tunnel route development for Island South. ML45 to ML84 went into operation from October 1987 to November 1988. The total 84 'Super Metrobuses' all had 'Gardner' 6LXCT turbo-charged engine with an output of 210bhp that comes with 'Voith' DIWA851 transmission system and full air suspension.

In the early days, 'Super Metrobuses' served on routes 90, 92, 96, 97, 99, 107 & 182. Later, they were commissioned on route 8, 42, 101, 104, 105, 10, 111, 112, 116, 690, 780 & 788.
