名稱：「利蘭」奧林比安型
Name : Leyland Olympian

車隊編號：BR
Fleet No: BR

內容簡介：

兩部『利蘭』「奧林比安型」兩軸樣版巴士，於八一年九月二十三日完成登記，加入中巴車隊成為BR1(CR4443)及BR2(CR4223)，兩部車的機械配搭是完全一樣，都是「吉拿」6LXB引擎、『利蘭』油壓全自動波箱及ZF轉向系統。此外，兩車均配用ECW的矮版車身，BR1 車身長度十米，BR2十點三米長，車身長短有別在於軸距。兩車座位編排同是二加二。

BR1在初出廠時被派往行走山頂15號路線作試驗，短時間後則與BR2同被安排到112路線服務，直至該線全部以三軸高載客量的巴士行走之後，再被編行走東區線，例如20、21及80 路線。

「奧林比安」的第二春發生於八九年，當兩車於春季進行驗車時，中巴管理層指示將兩車髹上豪華巴士顏色，又改用高背座椅，大家都以為被派往行走260及262路線。但改裝後，是與FC1、CM1及CM2被當作戰車，行走於八九年八月十二日起開辦的182路線，對抗城巴當時於八月十三日開辦的60R非專利路線(即後期的88R路線)。後來，BR1及BR2的原裝車頭水箱網已被更換，並同時編作後備車，於早上繁忙時間行走104及111路線。在九巴早已大量採用同品種『利蘭』「奧林比安」的時候，中巴這稀有巴士，特別受注目。而最令人津津樂道的是中巴繼續保持這款車的高背座椅及豪華巴士色彩。

Description :

The first two "Leyland" 'Olympian' 2-axle buses joined CMB on 23 September 1981 as BR1 (CR4443) & BR2 (CR4223) with identical specifications. They had 'Gardner' 6LXB engines, "Leyland" full-automatic hydraulic gearbox and ZF steering system. They also employed the low height ECW bodies. BR1 measured 10 Metre while BR2 was 10.3 Metre differing in their axle-distances. The same 2+2 seating arrangement applies to both buses.

In the early days, BR1 was tested on route 15 to the Peak. A short while later, it joined BR2 on route 112. After the route was completely taken over by 3-axle buses, they were arranged to Eastern District routes like 20, 21 & 80.

'Olympians' had a second chance in 1989 while the management of CMB instructed to repaint the 2 buses into Luxury buses with high-back seats initially thought to be used on routes 260 & 262. They were however used together with FC1, CM1 & CM2 as 'competition ones' on route 182 against Citybus's new route 60R (the exsisting route 88R) on 12 August & 13 August 1989 respectively. Later on, BR1 & BR2's original radiator grilles had been replaced and they were also used as reserve buses for the morning rush hour on route 104 & 111.

名稱：「利蘭」奧林比安型十一米
Name : Leyland Olympian 11 Metre

車隊編號：LM
Fleet No: LM

內容簡介：

相信這是中巴所訂購的最後十輛非空調巴士──『利蘭』「奧林比安」在九三年十二月登記。 新車出廠時，因車身供應商「亞歷山大」誤以為中巴會採納九巴同樣的單一幅數字布牌，故錯誤地提供有關零件，其後才得以更正，全數採用三幅獨立數字布牌，另外，中巴的「奧林比安」座位，放棄採用木板椅背，改用乳膠質料。蘋果綠色的扶手桿亦首次被採用於非空調巴士上。壓力感應器亦率先安裝在LM2的中門上，作為試驗。

為配合中巴的六十週年慶典花車巡遊活動，LM1及LM5於出廠後不久，分別髹上由『富豪』汽車及『加德士』石油氣所贊助的廣告，兩部車的前門及車尾引擎蓋上均有『VOLVO』的字樣，究其原因，原來「奧林比安」披上不同廣告後，便一一代表中巴出席各個有關的慈善宣傳活動，LM5是香港吸煙健康委員會，LM6醫院管理局，LM4及LM8則是世界野生動物基金會。

由於這款巴士的強大馬力，幾乎就是一部空調巴士的基本配搭──「康明斯」LTA引擎，配以ZF的4HP500全自動波箱，輸出動力綽綽有餘，所以全部被派往行走柴灣區8號路線及途經官塘繞道的680路線，但亦曾行走渣甸山的11號路線。在平時的日子，LM亦可見於馬鞍山的680路線、東隧的601路線及柴灣82號路線等。

Description :

The last 10 non air-conditioned "Leyland" 'Olympian' buses were believed to be the last of CMB's order. They were registered in December 1993. In the early delivery period, the supplier of 'Alexander' bodies mistakenly thought that CMB would use the same single unit for the Route indicator box as that in KMB and supplied the wrong parts. They were changed back to CMB's 3-pieces version later. Also the seating for all these 'Olympians' used foam-packed cushioned backs. The door handles were coloured apple-green. Pressure-sensor were also fitted on the middle door of LM2 for trial.

To celebrate CMB's 60th anniversary, LM1 & LM5 were decorated by "Volvo" cars and "Caltex" advertisement separately. Their front doors & the rear engines had however been fitted with a "Volvo" logo; it was because the 'Olympian' buses, with the different advertisements on its bodies, had represented CMB in the participation of various charity activities. LM5 was for the Anti-smoking Committee, LM6 for the Hospital Authority and LM4 & LM8 were for the World Wide Life Fund.

Because these model had high power, they were used mainly for route 8 on Chai Wan Road and route 680 via the Kwun Tong Bypass. Some had arrange to serve on route 11 up Jardine's Lookout.
