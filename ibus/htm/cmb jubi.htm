名稱：「丹尼士」喝釆型(又名「祖比倫型」)
Name : Dennis Jubilant

車隊編號：DS
Fleet No: DS

內容簡介：

在『利蘭』「勝利二型」出現在炮台山道後不久，『丹尼士』亦提供中巴一款在機械配搭、性能、甚至外貌等方面，均與「勝利二型」相似的「喝釆型」巴士，以爭取本港市場的地位。同樣，DS1(CD2198)在出廠初期，亦被派往服務41號路線，以測試其攀爬能力。

其後，全部三十輛「喝釆」亦被調至東區，作為8號及82號路線的主力，每日二十二小時，不停地上落柴灣道，並一直駐守東區，其中部份被調往服務80、81及82路線，但亦有被編作後備車。為減低駕駛艙內的溫度，中巴將駕駛艙內頁門改為搪門。

全部三十輛「喝釆型」巴士，均裝有「亞歷山大」車身，外表與「勝利二型」沒多大分別，但如果仔細察看，則可以從其較低短的駕駛座位，去辨別這是「喝釆型」來。

Description :

After 'Victory Mk II' appeared on Battery Hill Road, "Dennis" also introduced the 'Jubilant' to compete for the bus market in Hong Kong. The 'Jubilants' had similar mechanical specifications, performance and outlook as the 'Victory Mk IIs'. It followed therefore that the first DS1 (CD2198) was also tested on route 41 for its climbing capabilities.

Later on, all the 30 'Jubilant' buses were commissioned to the Eastern District to serve route 8 & 82, climbing up Chai Wan Road virtually 22 hours a day. The 'Jubilants' mainly served the Eastern District on routes 80, 81 & 82 for a long time, but some had been converted into reserves. To further reduce the driver's cabin temperature, CMB converted sliding doors to them.

All the 30 'Jubilant' buses came with 'Alexander' bodies and look similar to the 'Victory Mk IIs'. You can only differentiate the two by the lower driver's cabin of the 'Jubilants'.

CMB's 'Jubilant' DS1 was seen on Braemar Hill Road and not in Chai Wan.
