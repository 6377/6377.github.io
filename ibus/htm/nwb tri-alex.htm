名稱：「丹尼士」三叉戟型十點六米、十一點三米及十二米(「亞歷山大ALX500」車身)
Name : Dennis Trident 10.6 Metre, 11.3 Metre and 12 Metre ('Alexander ALX500' bodywork)

內容簡介：

新巴首批從英國『丹尼士』公司訂購的巴士車隊，其中包括160輛配有「亞歷山大ALX500」車身的「三叉戟型」十點六米、十一點三米及十二米雙層空調巴士。

跟其他本港專利巴士公司比較，新世界第一巴士服務有限公司的購車策略來得更加清晰。它跟其合資夥伴英國第一巴士公司集團的取向一致，就是購入低地台型號的全新巴士。因此，新巴向廣大市民，包括傷健人仕作出承諾，提供安全、可靠及物有所值的高質素服務。所有新購巴士均配備方便傷健人仕的設施，這些設施均符合英國殘疾人仕交通諮詢委員會(DPTAC)所釐定的標準，包括有：

  1. 車頭裝有顯眼的目的地及路線編號顯示牌。車身及背後均有路線編號。
  2. 低地台設計，上落口不設梯級，讓輪椅直達車廂。
  3. 入口設可收合斜台，由車長控制，方便輪椅上落，車廂內有指定停放輪椅空間。
  4. 特別的扶手粗幼及使用的物料，方便握扶。
  5. 鮮色的扶手顏色以方便弱視乘客使用。
  6. 經改良的停車按鈕，方便客使用。
  7. 上層及下層均設有“巴士將停站”顯示燈號，以方便聽覺較弱的乘客。
  8. 中門設有「請勿下車」顯示燈號及提示燈響號，提醒弱視和弱聽的乘客，巴士車門即將關閉。
  9. 所有出入口及梯級邊緣均有鮮明的標貼，以清楚顯示位置。
  10. 車廂設計寬敞，並設有優先予長者及傷健人仕優先使用的座位。

新巴低地台巴士的車廂內，每邊都採用雙座位設計，座椅寬敞舒適。

此外，全新的巴士都設有雙向無電通訊系統，方便車長與廿四小時車務中心聯絡。並採用節省能源及少廢氣的環保引擎。新車還設有速度限制儀器，以監控行車速度。

在已訂購的202輛『丹尼士』「三叉戟型」低地台巴士中，新巴選取兩家不同車身供應商，其中160輛「亞歷山大」車身分別在蘇格蘭Falkirk的總廠及在中國廈門的合資廠房，替新巴的『丹尼士』「三叉戟型」雙層巴士底盤，組裝最新的ALX500型車身。

新巴『丹尼士』「三叉戟型」巴士以紫色為主。首輛全新『丹尼十』「三叉戟」巴士在一九九八年七月七日抵港，編號1001。

除首輛1001的「三叉戟」是配用ZF全自動波箱外，其餘採用「福伊特」的D863.3全自動波箱，動力來源是「康明斯」M305E列式六汽缸渦輪增壓柴油引擎，最大輸出馬力有305bhp。

Description :

British bus manufacturer "Dennis" has won an order from New World First Bus to supply 278 air-conditioned buses for Hong Kong. The order is the first bus order placed by the new company, which started operating on 1st September 1998.

The buses are new generation low-floor designs, including 160 3-axle "Dennis" 'Trident' 12 Metre double-deckers with 'Alexander' body.

The first New World First Bus 'Alexander ALX500' bodied "Dennis" 'Trident' arrived in Hong Kong on 7th July 1998.

The bus carries wheelchair friendly features such as low floor with step free fold out ramps and flat access to the saloon for a wheelchair. These features meet the UK Disaled Persons Transport Advisory Committee standards. Moreover all the new buses are powered by environmentally friendly engines with high fuel efficiency and low gas emissions.

NWFB is committed to provide safe, high quality and good value services for the travelling public, including those who are less able. All the new buses are equipped with the following features:

  1. Large front destination display destination with route numbers at the side and rear.
  2. Low entrance and exit steps.
  3. Step free lower saloon floor.
  4. Suspension kneeling system.
  5. Wheel chair accessibility with entrance fold out ramp and flat access to priority bay. This has back-rest support and a signal bell for the driver.
  6. Special handrail support to assist less able passengers as they enter and move within vehicle. Additional vertical handrails in both saloons.
  7. Handrails are of large diameter textured materials to assist grip.
  8. Bus stop bell pushes are of improved design.
  9. Handrails are of bright contrasting colour to assist those with impaired eye-sight.
  10. Bus stopping signs in both decks that illuminate to confirm to those with that a poor hearing that stop signal has been given.
  11. All entrance and interior steps have bright contrasting "Tiger stripe" edges to clearly indicate the position of steps.
  12. Priority seats for aged and less able passengers. Wider spacing and additional handrails.

All these New World First Bus' fully air-conditioned and super low-floor buses were phased into service in 1998 and early 1999.
