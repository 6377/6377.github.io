城巴─非一般的公共運輸機構
Citybus - A Special Public Transport Company

城巴是本港一家非常特別的公共運輸機構，旗下的巴士服務範圍包括中港直通巴士、屋邨巴士服務以及專利巴士服務。同時，它的巴士車隊組合亦是別無他家的。

若論車隊的數目，城巴的車隊雖然並非全港之冠，但卻擁有為數不少的雙層空調巴士，而空調巴士在整個車隊中所佔的比例，卻成為全港之冠。

透過這個網頁，希望各位巴士迷哥迷姐更加了解這支既年輕，而又充滿活力的車隊狀況。

Citybus is unique among the Hong Kong public transport service sector in providing service across the Chinese border, to housing estates and franchised routes. The combination of Citybus fleet is also quite distinct from other competitors.

In terms of the number of its buses, it may not be the most, but has the most of all the air-conditioned double-decker. Citybus also has the highest ratio of such air-conditioned buses of all other competitors.

It is hope that all bus fans can fully understand this young and highly energetic bus company through this website.
