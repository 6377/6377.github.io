名稱：「丹尼士」三叉戟型十點三米及十二米(「都普Metsec」車身)
Name : Dennis Trident 10.3 Metre and 12 Metre ('Duple Metsec' bodywork)

內容簡介：

新巴首批從英國『丹尼士』公司訂購的巴士車隊，其中亦同時包括了42輛配有「都普Metsec」車身的「三叉戟型」十點三米及十二米雙層空調巴士。

「都普Metsec」車身的「三叉戟」一九九八年十一月付運抵港。全部新車都是採用「福伊特」的D863.3全自動波箱。動力來源是「康明斯」M305E列式六汽缸渦輪增壓器柴油引擎，最大輸出馬力有305bhp。車身是在英格蘭Wigan的Northern Counties車身的工場裝嵌。裝嵌完成後，被運往香港，並編上3001-3042的車隊編號。

這批新車在一九九九年十一月投入服務，行走590路線的3001，更披上「第一百架新巴」的裝飾，而其他「都普Metsec」車身的「三叉戟」相若，但車尾電子牌被加大，下層太平門的窗之己寸與其他車窗保持一致，車內的佈置和設備全是採用標準的，但車上的座椅被改成第一巴士的標準座椅，與其他「三叉戟」有所不同。

Description :

New World First Bus quickly placed orders for 202 'Tridents' when it was awarded the former CMB franchise. Of these 42 were bodied with 'Duple Metsec' kits in the UK at Plaxton (Wigan).

'Duple Metsec' bodied 'Trident' were arrived in Hong Kong in Novenber 1998. The 3-axle "Dennis" 'Tridents' were fitted with the 'Cummins' M305E (305bhp) engine linked to a 'Voith' D863.3 fully automatic gearbox.

The first 'Duple Metsec' 'Trident' was arrived in Hong Kong for NWFB was also the '100th new bus' for the company and 3001 proudly advertises this fact. New World First Bus has separated the 'Alexander' and 'Duple Metsec' 'Tridents' into different classes.

The 1999 orders for NWFB 'Tridents' call for 4 different lengths and 2 different body styles, includings 10.3 Metre and 12 Metre with 'Duple Metsec' bodywork.

The first "Dennis" 'Trident' with 'Duple Metsec' body, was completed the bodywork at Northern Counties at Wigan, England.
