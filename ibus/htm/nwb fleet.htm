名稱：「丹拿/利蘭」珍寶型
Name : Daimler/Leyland Fleetline

車隊編號：LF/SF
Fleet No: LF/SF

內容簡介：

新巴車隊內的熱狗巴士中，『丹拿』「Fleetline珍寶型」LF及SF巴士堪稱是全港碩果僅存的了，總數共一百輛，其中包括七十二輛長陣LF及廿八輛短陣SF。

雖然在中巴的全盛時期，曾出現過不同的車身組合，但留存在新巴車隊中的「珍寶型」，已是清一色的「亞歷山大」車身。至於車身長度較短、有動力輔助轉向、全自動變速箱及全風式制動系統的SF型號，一直都是攀爬太平山的15號路線的主力。後來因維修及零件儲存問題，而被改成半自動波箱。這些SF短身「珍寶」，亦已陸續功成身退。

Description :

New World First Bus non air-conditioned bus fleet that purchsed from CMB, including 72 "Daimler" 'Fleetline' long chasis LF and 28 short chasis SF.

The ex-CMB "Daimler" 'Fleetline' appeared with different types of bodywork, but they remained the same 'Alexander' bodywork in NWFB bus fleet. The shorter bodies SF were commissioned for climbing up the Peak on route 15. Later on, however, due to the shortage of spare parts and difficulties in maintanance, they were changed to the semi-auto standard of CMB buses. They were the first to phase from NWFB bus fleet within two years.

『丹拿』「Fleetline珍寶」LF26在七四年五月初投入服務，原配「Metal-Sections」車身，後於八八年換上「亞歷山大」車身，並於九八年九月繼續在新巴車隊內服務。
"Daimler" 'Fleetline' LF26 was originally fitted with 'Metal-Sections' body. It was rebodied with 'Alexander' bodywork in 1998 and joined the NWFB fleet on 1 Sept. 1998.

長度只有三十呎的標準版『丹拿』「珍寶型」巴士，特別適合港島區路窄多彎的山頂區路線。SF9這天不用攀登太平山，改在過海隧道線服務，途經太古城。
The ex-CMB short "Daimler" 'Fleetline' SF9, serves Cross Harbour Tunnel Route 110 heading for Kowloon. It had a body length of 30 feet.

這部前中巴的『丹拿』「珍寶型」巴士，在巴投入新車巴隊後，在尾引擎位置已貼上新巴的廣告標貼。
The ex-CMB "Daimler" 'Fleetline' had a special advertisement of NWFB on its rear engine.

SF16貼上新巴的宣傳廣告，仍然跟往日一樣，每天在上午繁忙時間趕往九龍紅磡隧道口「打仗」，支援中區路線。
SF6 showing the short last upper-deck bay of the LF-class is omitted and was a quick means of recognition.
『丹拿』「珍寶型」SF5已換上新巴的綠、白及橙色彩，行走88號線，前往中環方向。
'Fleetline' SF5 had a brand new NWFB livery, it served on route 88.
另一輛『丹拿』「珍寶型」SF7身披新巴的新色彩，正前往樁坎角方向。
Another 'Fleetline' SF7 was running towards Chung Hum Kok with a newly NWFB livery.
