名稱：「丹尼士」長茅型
Name : Dennis Lance

車隊編號：AN
Fleet No: AN

內容簡介：

想當年，九巴於一九八六年至八七年間引進首批『豐田』廿四座位單層空調巴士，行走當年往來調景嶺與彩虹的90號路線，其後又有『三菱』和『丹尼士』「飛驃」、「長茅」的出現.......。雖然，「長茅」的七十八人載客量，屬全港專利巴士中最高載客量的單層巴士車種，但客量的需求不斷轉變，亦證明了單層巴士的載客量實在不能勝任。所以，九巴於九十年代中期便開始全力發展和購入全新雙層空調巴士。

除原本的單門版本AN11、AN13至AN24仍然行走啟德機場「通天巴士」路線外，其餘雙門版本的「長茅」巴士亦由30X及238X線被抽調出來。可能是九巴的九龍灣廠內設有『丹尼士』工場，及方便與行走機場「通天巴士」的「飛驃」與「長茅」巴士統一維修，少部份原屬荔枝角廠的「長茅」巴士亦被派駐九龍灣廠，行走富山(瓊東街)及佐敦道碼頭的203E路線。如此一來，只餘下多輛留駐荔枝角廠，除用作後備車，支援青衣一帶路線外，有兩部則行走筲箕灣與佐敦碼頭的110路線。

Description :

The first "Toyota" 24 seater air-conditioned buses introduced by KMB in 1986 and 1987 to run route 90 between Rennie's Hill and Choi Hung. Later on, they were joined by "Mitsubishi" and "Dennis" 'Darts' and 'Lance' buses. The 'Lance' model has 78 seats and has the highest passenger-carrying capacity of any single-deck bus of the franchised fleets.

The original single door AN13 to AN24 served the 'Airbus' routes for Kai Tak Airport. The remaining dual-door 'Lance' buses were also withdrawn from routes 30X and 238X. It is likely, as Kowloon Bay Depot has facilities for servicing the "Dennis" buses, a small number of 'Lance' buses from the Lai Chi Kok Depot were redeployed to Kowloon Bay Depot to run route 203E between Fu Shan (King Tung Street) and Jordan Road Pier. There remain therefore only a few reserve buses at Lai Chi Kok Depot and 2 for route 110 between Shau Kei Wan and Jordan Road Pier.

The KMB's "Dennis" 'Lance' single-deck air-conditioned dual-door bus passed the Transport Department's tilt test on 19 May 1993. The passenger capacity of the new bus was authorized to 45 seatings and 33 standees.
