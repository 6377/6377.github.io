名稱：「利蘭」亞特蘭大型
Name : Leyland Atlantean

內容簡介：

這是被人稱為「黃老太」的城巴巴士。形容詞既親切，又形象得很。

一九九二年中，城巴成功地獲港府批准經營廿六條專利巴士線，但卻要在一年時間內，籌措二百餘輛巴士投入服務，這實在是一項大挑戰。最後，城巴一方面斥巨資訂購全新空調巴士，而另一方面則尋求非空調巴士，後者的出路，便是『利蘭』「亞特蘭大型」巴士了。

為迎接「Network 26新里程」，城巴向新加坡訂購的一百輛『利蘭』「亞特蘭大型」(行內人俗稱「坡柴」)雙層非空調巴士終在九三年夏季前付運抵港，工程人員連夜為巴士進行改裝及翻新，以求能取得「證書」為市民服務。事實上，翻新及改裝的費用不菲，改裝項目包括：翻新底盤及地台支架、更換新地台板、車身骨架的防霉處理、以至翻新或更換『利蘭』680引擎、SCG350波箱及尾軸的所有零件。而最為矚目的是換上高科技時代感──「亞特蘭大」車頭的所有纖維構件。

首部改裝成功編號602的「亞特蘭大」巴士於九三年五月十二日領得牌照。而隨後的600亦被改為編號333，作為宣傳「Network 26新里程」的「新聞稿」相片之用。新車最初被派往行走5、5A、5B、48、70M、72A、75、92、96及98路線。

「黃老太」畢竟已有十數年的車齡，車上又沒有空調，因此，城巴早已計劃於一年時間內，陸續淘汰「黃老太」。

Description :

To meet the requirement for non air-con buses to commence operation of 'Network 26' in September 1993, a total of 100 'Atlantean', to become 600-699 were acquired from Singapore Bus Services. The first vehicle SBS5365R was delivered in 1992 and refurbished as the prototype for the rest of the batch. Extensive refurbishment included the removal of all but the roof panels, and any flooring/seats/pillars/panels that required replacement were so treated. In addition, mechanical work was also undertaken, and finally an 'Alexander R' type front was fitted. Initially the prototype carried the lucky number 333, before renumbering in 1993 to 600. Upon delivery of the last vehicle it was again renumbered, this time to 602 to take its correct place in the fleet in chasis number order.

Although 100 vehicles were purchased, one with 'Alexander R' type body was scrapped to provide a source of spare parts. Of the remaining 99 vehicles, three vehicles , 628, 660 and 699 were converted to training buses and were given only mechanical overhauls along with the provision of an instructor's seat and licenced as Private Buses. These can stil lbe easily recognised as 628 and 660 carry the original 'Belfast' style front end as delivered to Singapore while 699 has an authentic 'Alexander R' type body. All the remaining vehicles entered passenger service after refurbishment, and have proven to be sturdy and reliable, contrary to the concern shown by those remembered previous generations of the 'Atlantean' in Hong Kong, In order to meet the need requirement for additional drivers in advance of the introduction of an additional 14 routes in 1995, two refurbished vehicles, 602 and 603 were withdrawn from the passenger fleet and converted to training buses.

During early 1996 the process of withdrawal commenced, however there has been considerable interest shown by operators worldwide in these extensively refurbished vehicles.
