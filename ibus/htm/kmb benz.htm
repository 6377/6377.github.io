內容簡介：

九巴車隊旗下為數四十一輛的『平治』非空調巴士，擁有悅耳的引擎聲音。不過，踏入九七年後，要欣賞這些悅耳噪音，則請移玉步到大埔、粉嶺及上水一帶了。

『平治』「0305型」雙層巴士是九巴購買的最後一款兩軸車款。其實，按這款德國巴士卓越的攀爬斜坡能力，應能在九巴車隊內大有作為，但因九巴在八十年代中已落實購買三軸巴士的取向，故未有再增購。

『平治』「0305型」雙層巴士採用無大樑式的底盤支架設計(俗稱「牙簽陣」)，載客量達一百二十三人；並沒有配備渦輪增壓的OM407h傳統呼吸引擎，發出210hp的最大馬力，提供驚人攀爬斜坡表現，加上車身被髹上奪目的色彩，成為八十年代中至末期九巴車隊的「車皇」。當年所有『平治』巴士，均被派出服務往來屯門公路的路線，它們在公路上動輒輕鬆地越過其他前置引擎的「勝利二型」巴士，故深受乘客歡迎。

當三號幹線及大欖隧道在一九九八年通車後，九巴已將68X線改繞新路和環迴公路往返元朗。另外，該線己全數以空調巴士行走，所以九巴已由九六年起，部署將大部份「『平治』雙軸巴士抽離68X線，並調往行走70、70K、70X、71A、71K、72A、73A、74A、74K、75K及76K路線，以配合新界北區「勝利二型」退役的時間表。至一九九七年八月為止，68X線只餘下兩部『平治』巴士。

Description :

A number of 41 "Mercedes Benz" '0305' ME double-deck buses of KMB come with a harmonious engine sound. The sad thing is, you have to move to the border area at Tai Po, Fanling and Sheung Shui to enjoy this tune after 1997.

"Mercedes Benz" '0305' ME double-deck buses were the last batch of 2-axle buses acquired by KMB. They have very good hill climbing capability. However, KMB decided in the mid-80s to adopt the 3-axle ones and therefore, there was no more addition of the "Mercedes Benz" buses.

The "Mercedes Benz" '0305' ME buses use the wide monocoque chasis with 123 passenger carrying capacity. They are fitted with the OM407h natural intake engine without turbocharger but still delivering 210hp. They also take the slopes with ease and together with the bright colours of the bodies, they were the 'King' of the KMB buses in the mid and late '80s.

After the new Route 3 and Tai Lam Tunnel had opened in 1998, KMB re-routed 68X to take the new Route 3 and Tai Lam Tunnel, and the trunk roads to Yuen Long with air-conditioned buses. From 1996 onwards, KMB withdrew most of "Mercedes Benz" 2-axle buses from route 68X and transferred them to routes to Tai Po area and the New Territories North including routes 70, 70K, 70X, 71A, 71K, 72A, 73A, 74A, 74K, 75K & 76K to tie in with the decommissioning of the 'Victory Mk II' buses. By August 12 1997, there remained only 2 "Mercedes Benz" buses for 68X.
