Name : Dennis Dart

內容簡介：

由於新巴所接辦經營的路線，不及前中巴多，所以自新巴投入服務後，購入自中巴的共一百九十九輛空調巴士行走的路線網可以更集中，感覺上新巴的空調巴士不見得顯著短缺。況且，新巴將全新剛運抵港的其他低地台空調巴士，連同這些二手車一起，實現以往港島區居民難得一見的全完調巴士服務路線。例如，從九月一日起，新巴4X、8P和M21等，以及原有的19及78號線。另外，這些新舊合併的新巴空調巴士陣容，亦同時加密海隧線的冷氣巴士班次，令乘客全面受惠。

這一百九十九輛空調巴士中，『丹尼士』「飛驃型」DC單層九點八米空調巴士共佔二十輛。在新巴全面進行路線空調化計劃期間，擔綱的車款既有舊車，亦有新車，當中，剛於九月中旬實現空調化的13號路線，便是以舊款的『丹尼士』「飛驃型」DC單層九點八米空調巴士，這些配以清一色「Carlyle」車身的『丹尼士』「飛驃」，駛上港島半山區，直達旭和道，它們途經這些早已被別家專利巴士公司覆蓋的區域時，無論是車身顏色，甚或車款，都顯得非常突出。

新巴首輛『丹尼士』「飛驃」SLF特低地台單層空調巴士，於一九九八年七月三十一日運抵香港，帶來前所未見的『Plaxton Pointer二型」車身。它的首個車程是在一九九八年九月一日凌晨，行走新旵號路線的頭班車，從港澳碼頭出發，前往筲箕灣。

Description :

New World First Bus launched all 52 of its own brand new buses include 33 'Plaxton Pointer' 2-bodied "Dennis Dart" SLFs and 19 "Alexander" ALX500-bodied "Dennis" 'Tridents' for early morning services on 1st September 1998. But of the 710 CMB buses it bought for HK$335 million, only 199 are air-conditioned buses.

Of the list of 199 air-conditioned buses purchased from CMB, they include 20 "Dennis" 'Dart' DCs. In April 1991, "Dennis" offered Hong Kong's first 9.8 Metre "Dennis" 'Dart' DC1 for trial runs by CMB. Then CMB made a further order for 19 'Darts' to strengthen its luxury bus fleet. They have 'Carlyle' bodywork and were used on route 260.

Later on, they were arranged to serve on route 13, after NWFB acquired the franchise from the government in Mid-September 1998. New World First Bus invested a total of HK$2 billion in various essential operating facilities including 500 brand new air-con buses, a permenant depot and other passenger facilities.

British Bus manufacturer "Dennis" has won an order from NWFB to supply 278 air-con buses for Hong Kong. The order is the first bus order placed by the new company, which started operating on 1st September 1998.

The buses are new generation low-floor designs, which includes 42 10.7Metre 'Plaxton Pointer 2' bodied 'Dart' SLF and 34 10.1 Metre 'Plaxton Pointer 2' bodied "Dennis" 'Dart' SLF single deckers.

The first "Dennis" 'Dart' SLF of New World First Bus Services Ltd., 2001 had arrived in Hong Kong on 31st July 1998 with "Plaxton Pointer 2' bodywork. Its first duty was carried out of 00:05 am on 1st September 1998 serving on route 2, departure from Macau Ferry Pier.
