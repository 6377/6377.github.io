名稱：「丹尼士」飛驃型
Name : Dennis Dart

車隊編號：DC/CX
Fleet No: DC/CX

內容簡介：

一九九一年四月，『丹尼士』提供了全港首部九點八米版的「飛驃型」DC1給中巴試用。引擎是「康明斯」6BT型號，波箱是「Allison」的AT545四前速全自動型號，車身由「卡賴爾」供應，配合「超卓」空調系統，全車載客量共五十九人，包括十六個企位，十足一輛現代版的豪華巴士。

DC1一出廠便派往行走260路線，車資十元。同時，中巴再增購十九輛「飛驃」，以鞏固其現代化的豪華巴士車隊。DC2至DC20於九一年九月至九二年期間付運抵港，並分別派往服務空調化的260路線、混於「都城」巴士中行走的261及262路線及全新開辦的525、590、592及595路線，奇怪的是，空調化後的260路線，車資竟減低，全程只收八元。

九四年八月，中巴再加訂八輛配上「馬素」車身的「飛驃」CX1至CX8，在三號風球高懸下抵港。中巴同時將DC1刪減前車軸蓋上的六個座位，改裝上行李架，以便配合開辦新的機場巴士路線。經過一年時間與運輸署斡旋，中巴機場巴士A20路線最終在九五年九月十七日上午六時開出首班車，再加上經改裝的DC2至DC4，便組成了一支擁有十二輛「飛驃」的機場巴士車隊。中巴另外又在同年的十一月更改了CX3、CX7及CX8的車牌，「新」的車牌號碼分別為BJ825、CJ790及CX908。「飛驃」的「耙」亦仍可在15、43X、84M、110、262、500、504及537路線找到。

Description :

In April 1991, "Dennis" offered the Hong Kong's first 9.8 Metre 'Dart' DC1 for trail runs by CMB. The engines was from 'Cummins' 6BT, the transmission was a 4-speed full-automatic AT545 from 'Allison', the body from 'Carlyle' and the air-conditioning from 'Sutrak'. The total carrying capacity was 59 with 16 standing. For all intents and purposes, it was therefore a luxury bus by all standards. DC1 was therefore immediately put to use on route 260 and the fare was $10. At the same time, CMB ordered an additional 19 'Dart' to strengthen its luxury bus fleet. DC2 to DC20 were delivered between September 1991 and February 1992. They were used on routes 260. Some were mixed with the 'Metrobuses' on routes 261 & 262. Additional routes also include 525, 590, 592 & 595. Surprising enough, the air-conditioned route 260 charges $8 only.

In August 1994, the 8 'Dart' buses with 'Marshall' bodies ordered by CMB (CX1 - CX8) arrived in Hong Kong under Typhoon signal No.3. In additional to them, CMB also removed the 6 seats at the front of DC1 to be fitted with luggage racks so as to turn them into Airport Buses. It took over a year to finally obtain the approval from Transport Department to launch the Airport bus service of CMB route A20 at 6 am on September 1995. With the conversion of DC2 to DC4, the Airport bus fleet consists of a total number of 12. Later on, CMB also re-registered CX3, CX7 & CX8 in November into BJ825, CJ790 & CX908. Some of the remaining 'Darts' could be found on routes 43X, 84M, 110, 262, 500, 504 & 537.
