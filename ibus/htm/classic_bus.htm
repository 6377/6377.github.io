即使已經對現代巴士有一個全面的認識，又或者是剛開始去認識現代巴士，以下的內容，是可以加深對古老巴士(又或者稱之為「古典巴士」)各個範疇的了解。

巴士底盤：

幾乎所有的古典巴士的製造，亦即是從最古老的英國巴士開始，無論是單層，還是雙層，一切都是從底盤開始。首先是造好一個完整的底盤，才裝嵌整個車體。

在底盤上面，車子的一切動力組合，如引擎、變速箱(又稱為「波箱」)、車軸、車輪等，都一應俱全。完成後的巴士底盤，當然還包括有油缸、儀錶板、呔盤，及簡單的駕駛座椅。即是說，一台完成出廠的底盤，只是欠缺容納乘客的車廂而已。

動力組合：

傳統的英國巴士，引擎都是放在車頭的，經數十年的演變，後置引擎成為主流。前置引擎的底盤佈局，再配合半駕駛艙式的車身設計，成為四十至六十年代英國巴士最獨特的設計配搭，從現代人的眼光來看，它們全部都是充滿個性的巴士。

變速箱：

早期的英國巴士，其變速箱的操作方式，是最被駕駛者嘖有煩言的對象。古老的英國巴士，多選用非同步齒合波箱(Constant Mesh)，後來演進至先選式波箱(Preselective)，都屬於花氣力操縱的變速系統。直至出現半自動波箱(俗稱「電波」)之後，司機才算鬆一口氣，在駕駛巴士時才算得上是有點輕鬆的感覺。至於近年所流行的全自動波箱，在四十、五十年代簡直只是一個夢想而已。

車體尺寸：

從英國引進的單、雙層巴士，初期完全是沿用英國的車體尺寸規格，以雙層巴士長度為例，從四十年代的26呎，到五十年代的30呎，都是拜英放寬條例所。直至六十年代，回應本港環境的需要，才出現一套本地的公共巴士車體尺寸的條例，令巴士的長度，能夠較英國本土更加寬鬆。而現行條例，巴士的闊度以2.5米為限，雙層巴士的高度不能超過4.6米。至於車身的長度，則以12米為限。

It matters not whether you are already familiar with the modern buses or just starting to get to know them, the following will enable you to understand in greater detail the classic ones as well.

Chasis:

Virtually all the classic buses started with British ones. The bodies, single-decked and double-decked, were built on the chassis.

On the chassis, all the power train like the engine, gear-box, axle and wheels were added. The finished chassis also has the fuel tank, instrument panel, steering wheel and a driver's seat. In simple terms, a completed chassis could be driven on the road except for the fitting of the passenger cabin.

Power train:

The British buses had their engines at the front traditionally. After decades of modifications, rear engine now have become the norm. The layout of front engines with a semi-detached driver's compartment were standard for the British buses in the '40s to '60s. In today's eye , they are really unique.

Gear-box:

In the old days, the operation of the gear-box was a major complaint about British buses. The classic British buses had constant mesh gear-boxes. Later, they were replaced by preselective ones; all requiring substantial strength to operate. After the appearance of semi-automatic gear-boxes, the bus driver's job started to ease. The present fully automatic transmission was a dream in those days in the '40s and the '50s.

Bus Measurements:

All the buses imported from England adopted their British specifications. The double-decked buses initially measured 26 ft. in length in the '40s and later was relaxed to 30 ft. in the '50s. From the '60s, a new code for public buses for Hong Kong's environment was adopted which allowed even greater flexibility than their UK counterpart. The present specifications allows a maximum width of 2.5 metre and a maximum height of 4.6 metre for doubled-decked buses; the overall length is limited to 12 metre.
