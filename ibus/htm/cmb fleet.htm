Name : Daimler/Leyland Fleetline

車隊編號：LF/SF
Fleet No: LF/SF

內容簡介：

首部全新後置引擎雙層巴士「珍寶型」SF1(AX6001)，於一九七二年抵港，帶來嶄新的雙層巴士設計，亦揭開了更新行走15號路線車隊的序幕。中巴為配合海隧通車及改變車種以便全面採用「一人控制模式」，於七三年至七九年間，一口氣購入三百三十五輛有「珍寶巴士」稱號的『丹拿』「Fleetline」，包括三百零五輛三十三呎長車陣的LF系列及三十輛三十一呎短車陣的SF系列。

首一百零六輛LF在新車出廠時是披上「Metsec」車身的，其餘的均是擁有「亞歷山大」車身外貌。但後期因為經過祝融垂青或原來的車身經已受損，有些已被新裝上「亞歷山大」車身，此外，車身顏色試驗於十多年前曾進行在「亞歷山大」車身上，但十多年後再次發生。九二年夏季，LF234的上半身被髹上淺奶白、下半身是兩級不同的淺藍色，而原來所有的黑色橫間則消失了。九三年底，LF67被髹上MC豪華巴士顏色，其後所有經常行走43X路線的都被髹上豪華的色彩。到九六年五月，中巴仍然擁有二百三十六輛LF。

車身長度較短、有動力輔助轉向、全自動傳動系統及全風式制動系統，都是SF2至SF31能夠成為當年南區6及61路線，以及攀上太平山的15號路線的台柱。後來因維修及零件儲存問題，而改成中巴半自動傳動系統標準。SF1及SF2 分別於八八年及八四年停止行走，而SF27亦因為在九五年四月三日的意外中，被城巴撞及車尾而被迫拆毀外，其餘二十八輛仍活躍於3A、15、15B、45、64、66、78及95B路線。

Description :

The first rear-engine double-decker bus SF1 (AX6001) first arrived in Hong Kong in 1972. It marked a new era of double-decker buses operate by the driver alone for the route 15 and Cross-Harbour Tunnel Routes. CMB bought from 1973 to 1979 a total of 335 "Jumbo" "Daimler" 'Fleetline' buses including 305 33-feet long-chasis LFs and 30 30-feet shorter SF ones.

The first 106 LF buses had 'Metsec' bodies while the remaining ones had 'Alexander' bodies. Some of the earlier models caught fire or were damaged and they were then refitted with 'Alexander' bodies as well. Also CMB introduced a colour scheme trial some 10 years ago but again another trial took place a decade later. In summer of 1992, the upper half of LF234 was repainted with a light cream colour, while the lower half had 2 tones of light blue. Gone however was the original black stripe on the body. At the end of 1993, LF67 was painted with the MC Luxury bus colour scheme and so were all the buses that served route 43X. CMB still had 236 LF buses until May 1996.

The sucess of SF2 to SF31 on route 6 & 61 serving the Island South as well climbing up the Peak on route 15 depands on their shorter bodies, power-steering, full-automatic transmission and full air-brakes. Later on, however, due to the shortage of spare parts and difficulties in maintanance, they were changed to the semi-auto standard of CMB buses. SF1 & SF2 were quitted in 1988 and 1984 respectively. SF27 was named a total loss after having been hit from the back by a Citybus on 3 April 1995. The remaining 28 SFs were still active on routes 3A, 15, 15B, 45, 64, 66, 78 & 95B.

名稱：「丹拿/利蘭」珍寶型DMS
Name : Daimler/Leyland Fleetline DMS

車隊編號：XF
Fleet No: XF

內容簡介：

一九八零初某天，一輛與「火柴盒」(Matchbox) Londoner合金玩具車一模一樣的雙層巴士，披著中巴車隊基本車身色彩，在港島中區海傍駛過。倫敦的「DMS型」巴士已正式入侵中巴車隊，合共二百零七輛，包括一輛被改裝的訓練巴士，並被安排接替部份原本由『佳牌』的M、S及LS型巴士所執行的較輕盈任務，在港島各區行走，更成為地鐵修正早期系統通車後，接駁金鐘站的主力，包括有5M、10M、11M、20M、21M、22M及23M。

XF206 (DD7785) 於八八年被改裝成一輛有高背座椅的豪華巴士，並重新命名為FC1，作為260及262路線的後備車。

中巴的XF (DMS) 巴士，全部來自倫敦，都是配用「Park Royal」的車身，由於車身結構不弱，而載客量又不高，乘坐舒適，所以受到乘客的歡迎。唯一的缺點，是在下雨天的時候，車廂內間中有小雨出現的情況。

由於年事高及載客量較其土產親屬(LF)低，中巴亦陸續放棄倫敦巴士，直至九六年五月底只登記十四輛，其中九輛(XF129、135、140、141、171、181、184、197及204)被列作後備車，行走2、10、84M及788路線。到九六年七月二十八日，所有倫敦巴士終於退役。猶記得九巴亦層在同一時間引進倫敦「珍寶DMS型」巴士，但「DMS」巴士再度煥發魅力的地區，實非港島莫屬。

Description :

One day in early 1980, a double-decker that looked exactly like the 'Matchbox' Londoner model with CMB colours running on the waterfront of Hong Kong Island. DMS buses from London already joined the CMB fleet, and together a total of 207 such London buses, including one converted as a training bus, were introduced to replace the "Guy" buses including their M, S & LS classes. They were used for light duties on the Island, and also serve as feeder buses for the shuttle routes for the Island MTR lines at Admiralty Station. They include routes 5M, 10M, 11M, 20M, 21M, 22M & 23M.

XF206 (DD7785) was converted into a Luxury bus with high-back seats in 1988, and renamed FC1. It was used as reserve bus for routes 260 & 262.

The XF (DMS) buses of CMB all came from London with 'Park Royal' bodies. They had strong bodies and because of their low passenger capacity, they were comfortable to be on. The only minor defect would be the drips inside the cabin while it rains.

With their increasing age and low passenger capacity than their fellow LFs, CMB continues to give up them later on. Until the end of May 1996, CMB had only registered 14 XF of which 9 (XF129, 135, 140, 141, 171, 181, 184, 197 & 204) were being used as reserve buses serving routes 2, 10, 84M & 788). All London buses were decommissioned from the CMB fleet by July 28 1996.
