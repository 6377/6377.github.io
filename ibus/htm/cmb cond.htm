名稱：「丹尼士」禿鷹型十二米(非空調)
Name : Dennis Condor 12 Metre (Non Air-conditioned)

車隊編號：DL
Fleet No: DL

內容簡介：

「九龍有巨龍，香港有禿鷹」。『丹尼士』於八二年中向中巴提供兩部「禿鷹型」十二米巴士，分別是裝上「都普Metsec」車身，載客量可達一百七十一人的DL1 (CV4990)，及「亞歷山大」車身，載客量可達一百六十四人的DL2 (CW3359)。DL1是全港載客量最高的巴士，可容納一百七十一人。

一如其他樣版車，兩車同樣被派往行走112號路線，不同的是年期較長，直至八九年底，DL1才被調離112路線，改為服務南區線，而DL2則被編作後備車。當中巴打算繼續向『都城嘉慕』購買「超級都城型」的時候，卻傳出『都城嘉慕』結束營業的消息，中巴唯有向其他的供應商招手，而早期的「禿鷹」表現尚算理想，加上同廠「統治者型」的合作，新車的訂單順理成章地落入『丹尼士』的手中。配用「都普Metsec」車身的四十六輛十一米「禿鷹」，於八九年七月至九零年一月期間登記，並被安排效力南區42路線、隧道102、111、112、116、182及601路線。新的「禿鷹」採用「吉拿」6LXCT渦輪增壓引擎，相比起舊「禿鷹」的6LXC引擎，有更大的馬力。而車廂內，又加設吹風機，以增加車內空氣的流動。後來，隨著不斷地加入空調巴士，令部份「禿鷹」被安排服務東區的主幹線──8號路線服務。中巴的『丹尼士』「禿鷹型」巴士在投入服務的初期，尤其是受到廣告客戶的歡迎，間接地令「禿鷹」成為中巴過海隧道幹線的首選。中巴「禿鷹」的整體車身雖與九巴的「巨龍」非常相似，但如仔細地留意，可從車子的加速性能、全部的通風裝置分辨出來。

Description :

"Dragon for Kowloon & Condor for Hong Kong Island". And so "Dennis" introduced to CMB in 1982 the DL1 (CV4990) with 'Duple Metsec' body - capacity 171, and the DL2 (CW3359) with 'Alexander' body - capacity 164. DL1 was the highest passenger capacity bus in Hong Kong and like all demonstration buses was used on route 112. it served until the end of 1989 and was deployed to route 90 served the Island South. DL2 was used as a reserve bus.

When CMB was about to place additional orders for more 'Super Metrobuses', "Metro Cammell" stopped production. Understandably with the satisfactory performance of the early 'Condors' and the co-operation of the 'Dominators', the orders went to "Dennis". The first batch of 46 12 Metre 'Condors' with 'Duple Metsec' bodies were registered between July 1989 and January 1990, they were arranged to serve on route 42 for the Island South and Tunnel Routes 102, 112, 116, 182 and 601. The new 'Condors' used 'Gardner' 6LXCT turbo-charged engines with more power than the older 6LXC engines. The additional ventilation blowers were installed to improve air circulation inside the bus. Later on, with the introduction of air-conditioning buses, a number of 'Condors' were used mainly for route 8 for the Eastern District.

Although 'Condors' looked like 'Dragon', there were some differences between their ventilating and accelerating system.

中巴的DL1擁有一百七十一人的載客量，令「禿鷹型」將「巨龍」比下去了。
CMB's 'Condor' DL1 had a capacity of 171, and made a record in Hong Kong since 1982.

中巴的DL2是唯一一輛裝有「亞歷山大」車身的「禿鷹型」巴士，載客量不及DL1，只有載客量164人。
CMB's DL2 was an 'Alexander' R-type bodied bus and this combination remained unique in the CMB.

DL2的車身配搭，在中巴的車隊之中，顯得別樹一幟。
DL2 was a rare case in CMB fleet.

DL3是在兩部樣版車DL1及DL2投入服務的七年之後才出現的，亦是中巴選購的第二款三軸巴士。
CMB "Dennis" 'Condor' DL3 entered service in 1989, and served on route 42.

中巴DL3裝置有車廂通風系統，並常見服務於南區42號路線。
CMB "Dennis" 'Condor' DL3, was seen in service on 42, and fitted with additioal ventilation system.

DL4成為東區主幹線──8號路線的主力。
CMB DL4 was a strong one for route 8 which was a trunk route in Eastern District.

名稱：「丹尼士」禿鷹型十一米(非空調)
Name : Dennis Condor 11 Metre (Non Air-conditioned)

車隊編號：DM
Fleet No: DM

內容簡介：

中巴繼於兩年內購入共八十六輛十二米巴士後，再緊接購買入十二輛空調巴士，之後再於九零年底推出二十八輛十一米非空調的「禿鷹型」巴士投入服務。這些非空調「禿鷹」的外貌及內部佈置，與空調版的「禿鷹」幾乎完全相同，但梗窗變成搪窗；空調機變吹風機；乳膠椅背變成木板椅背。載客人數則較空調的稍多，為一百四十二人。這批「禿鷹」巴士，中巴採用了較新的「都普Metsec」車身，完全擺脫九巴所選用同廠車身的不對稱格調。而從這款車身開始，成為九巴同款巴士的標準車身。

引擎方面，則選用「吉拿」6LXCT型，再配合「福伊特」DIWA851傳動組合，上山及落斜都有十足馬力。因此，十一米「禿鷹」行走的路線離不開長命斜路，早期有行經鴨洲橋及利東的90及97號路線、途經柴灣道的780及不時與九巴一起在窩打老道或寶琳道斜路競賽的182及690路線；以及後期的2A、8、23及619(119)路線等，都成為中巴「禿鷹」的首選。

這些非空調版「禿鷹」，幾乎可以肯定的是最後一批由『丹尼士』提供的非調型號。隨著中巴後期傾向購入更多的空調巴士，及「禿鷹」底盤停產，非空調「禿鷹」新車已不復見。

Description :

In addition to the purchase in 2 years a total of 86 12 Metre buses, CMB also bought the first batch of 12 air-conditioned ones. Then at the end of 1990, CMB introduced 28 11 Metre non air-conditioned 'Condor'' buses. They looked virtually the same as their air-conditioned counterparts. The differences were the sliding windows for the fixed ones, fans for the air-conditioning unit and wooden backs for the cushioned seats. The passenger capacity was slightly more than the air-conditioned model at 142.

As for the engine, they used the 'Gardner' 6LXCT engines with 'Voith' DIWA851 transmission, more than adequate to climb steep gradients. They were therefore mostly used for routes that have long climbs like route 90 and 97 that go through the Apleichau Bridge and Lee Tung Estate, route 780 via Chai Wan Road, routes 182 via Waterloo Road and route 690 via Po Lam Road. Later on, they were also used on routes 2A, 8, 23 and 619(119).

These non air-conditioned 'Condor' buses were the last production of "Dennis" non air-conditioned buses. With CMB's air-conditioning modernization plan and the cessation of 'Condor' chasis production, there was no more of them to be seen.

中巴「禿鷹」 DM28 滿載著乘客，途經紅棉道，向蒲飛路前進。
CMB's 'Condor' DM28 was running via Cotton Tree Drive, towards Pokfield Road.

DM1是中巴的首部非空調『丹尼士』「禿鷹」巴士，車身是由「都普Metsec」所供應。
DM1 was the first non air-condtioned "Dennis" 'Condor' bus with 'Duple Metsec' bodywork.
中巴的「禿鷹」DM10非空調巴士擁有同廠十二米「禿鷹」的動力配搭──「吉拿」6LXCT引擎，所以在攀爬斜坡時，特別顯得動力十足。
CMB's 'Condor' 11 Metre non air-conditioned bus DM10 had 'Gardner' 6LXCT engine enough to climbing up to Yiu Tung Estate.

DM14駛進小西灣巴士總站，其十一米長的「都普」車身，很容易地從其身上的七隻車窗分辨出來。
DM14 was approaching Siu Sai Wan Terminus, the 11 Metre bus could easily recognised by its seven full-sized window bays.

DM2 was arranged to serve on route 601, and was running back to Chai Wan.
