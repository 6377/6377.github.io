名稱：「丹尼士」禿鷹型十一米(空調)
Name : Dennis Condor 11 Metre (Air-conditioned)

車隊編號：DA
Fleet No: DA

內容簡介：

『丹尼士』「禿鷹型」十一米DA是新世界第一巴士服務有限公司接手經營中巴的專利權後，向中巴購入的空調巴士之一，全數共九十二部。首十二部是於九零年七月至八月間投入服務，行走當年中巴的首條全空調巴士路線537，往來置富與金鐘之間。

『丹尼士』「禿鷹」的服務網非常廣闊，幾乎出現在每一條有空調巴士服務的新巴路線上，南至赤柱，北至沙田馬鞍山，而較突出的，首推由新巴獨家經營的西隧線970，幾乎是清一色由『丹尼士』「禿鷹」擔綱服務。

這九十二部『丹尼士』「禿鷹」空調巴士中，目前大部份車廂內中巴時代的3+2座椅設計，已轉為擁有新巴色彩的紫色2+2座椅設計，另外，車身亦換上了新巴的標準色彩。所有「禿鷹」在一九九八年九月一日全面投入服務。

Description :

After New World First Bus sucessfully acquired the franchise from the government, a number of 99 "Dennis" 'Condor' DA 11 Metre air-conditioned buses were purchased from CMB.

The first 12 "Dennis" 'Condor' air-conditioned buses with 'Duple Metsec' bodywork were hastily commissioned into service from July to August in 1990. The first air-conditioned route was 537 running between Admiralty and Chi Fu. These buses have 'Cummins' LT10 engines, the first for CMB. With the addition of the air-conditioning unit from 'Nippondenso' and the 3-speed fully-automatic transmission from 'Voith' DIWA863, they formed the basic specifications for all future air-conditioned buses.

Later on, they appeared with NWFB's standard colour. Most of them were to feature the more customer friendly 2+2 seating arrangement instead of 2+3 with a distinct colour - purple in colour. They were all commissioned on 1 September 1998.
