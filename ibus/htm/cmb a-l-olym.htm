名稱：「利蘭」奧林比安型十一米(空調)
Name : Leyland Olympian 11 Metre (Air-conditioned)

車隊編號：LA
Fleet No: LA

內容簡介：

中巴在九一年所訂購的新車名單中，除訂購二十四部空調『丹尼士』「禿鷹型」(DA)及二十部『丹尼士』「飛驃型」(DC)外，還特別訂購五部十一米空調『利蘭』「奧林比安型」(LA)，亦是中巴試驗了十年「奧林比安」後，首次購入同系車款。

中巴旗下的「奧林比安」空調巴士(LA)，外貌及內部設計是完全參照九廣鐵路公在當時所使用的「奧林比安」型號巴士，採用「亞歷山大」車身，而機械配搭則是其他同系車款的標準，「康明斯」LTA10引擎、ZF的4HP500全自動排檔裝置和「日本電裝」空調系統。

LA1至LA5於九一年十月至十二月間登記，車身顏色則沿用初期空調DA的款式，再加上車頭的一對霧燈及車頂首尾的兩對高顯示燈，顯得十分醒目。其後，中巴再增購二十部，並於九二年七月至十一月間付運及投入服務。由LA6(FF3170)出廠開始，中巴計劃更改空調巴士車身顏色，LA6以淺藍色為底色及在中層配以紅黃橫條；LA7至LA9及LA12至LA15是全部純以三級不同的淺藍色，上述的構思未能獲得中巴管理層所接受。終於，LA16還是以白色為主色調出現，最後，白色亦成為中巴雙層空調巴士的基本色彩，而原先的所有試驗色調，均被換為與LA16看齊。事實上，這樣的設計，不但保留中巴基本的藍色主調，亦予人清新的感覺。

Description :

Of the list of buses ordered by CMB in 1991, other than the 24 "Dennis" 'Condor' DAs and 20 "Dennis" 'Dart' DCs, there were also include 5 11 Metre air-conditioned "Leyland" 'Olympians'. These were CMB's first reorder of 'Olympians' after testing them for over 10 years.

The air-conditioned 'Olympian' LA buses all had the same outlook and interior design as the 'Olympians' of the KCRC. They had 'Alexander' bodies and standard 'Cummins' LTA10 engines, 4HP500 full-automatic transmission from ZF and 'Nippon Denso' air-conditoning units.

LA1 to LA5 were first registered from October to December 1991. They used the original DA air-conditioned model colours. They looked distinct with a pair of fog lights and the 2 pairs of height-marker lights in front and at the back. CMB then added another 20 of them and they were commissioned from July to November 1992. From LA6 (FF3170) onwards, CMB apparently changed its air-condtioned buses into a different colouring scheme. LA6 had light blue with red and yellow stripes at the middle. LA7 to LA9 and LA12 to LA15 had 3 different shades of light blue. All these colouring schemes have however not been accepted by CMB's management, and finally LA16 took on a white background colour and confirmed CMB's basic colour for its air-conditioned fleet. All the previous ones were subsequently repainted the same as LA16.
