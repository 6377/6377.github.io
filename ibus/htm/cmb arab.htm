名稱：「佳牌」亞拉伯五型
Name : Guy Arab MK V

車隊編號：LX
Fleet No: LX

內容簡介：

在七十年代初的全盛時期，中巴有超過五百輛同時在港島各區川流行走、及被冠上名稱為「紅番頭」的『佳牌』「亞拉伯五型」雙層巴士，隨著中巴的巴士網絡逐漸減少，終於面臨被淘汰的命運。

當時在服役中的「亞拉伯五型」，曾於八一年至八二年期間，被放棄了原本由「Metsec」所供應的車身外貌，重新裝上「亞歷山大」車身，以延長其服務的年期，擁有“半駕駛艙”的前置引擎車身設計。

其後，原本由「紅番頭」所行走的47及77號路線，已被其他車款所代替，同時亦逐步淡出途經大潭道的14號線。中巴於九四年底將LX316改裝成錢箱車，與已被改裝的LX26及LX290一起並肩，繼續服役穿梭各車廠。截至九六年五月底，中巴仍然登記有十輛「紅番頭」 巴士，但只有LX319及332仍經常被安排行走。

說起LX319及332的背景，前者原來是一九六六年九月抵港的新車，所採用的引擎是「吉拿」6LW，其後被換上6LX引擎，是編號LX142，在八一年時換上新車身，成為LX319。後者是原裝的LX版本，並在八二年更換車身，成為後期的樣子。

「紅番頭」最終在中巴車隊中絕跡。

Description :

The early 70s saw the maximum number of over 500 nick-named 'Red Indian Cap' 'Arab MK V' buses in the streets of Hong Kong . Until the reduction of the CMB network, these 'Arab MK Vs' had finally arrived at the end of their service.

At that time, 'Red Indian Cap' had their original 'Metsec' bodies been replaced by 'Alexander' bodies in 1981 and 1982 to extend their serviceable years. They were the only model of all franchised buses to have the front engine 'half-cab' design.

Later on, the 'Red Indian Cap' running on routes 47 & 77 were replaced by other models and they are also being withdrawn from route 14 on Tai Tam Road. CMB converted LX316 into a coin-collection bus in April 1996 to join the other LX261 & 290 running between the depots. Until May 1996, CMB still had 10 'Red Indian Cap' but only LX319 & 332 are operational.

Some interesting background information about LX319 & 332. The former first arrived brand-new in Hong Kong in September 1966 with a 'Gardner' 6LW engine, later changed to a 6LX engine and registered as LX142. It was replaced with a new body in 1981 and became LX319. The latter is the original LX model and the body was changed in 1982 until the end of her service.

Finally, the 'Red Indian Cap' disappeared from CMB fleet.
