內容簡介：

七九年十月初某一清晨，一輛簇新的『利蘭』「勝利二型」LV1(CA9169)，用著一鼓由「吉拿」6LXB引擎所發出的動力，負著過半百乘客，衝上險斜的炮台山道，沿41路線向著華富進發。從那時起，上落一比八的斜坡，已不再是『佳牌』「亞拉伯五型」巴士專美了。

中巴首一百四十七輛「勝利二型」是採用「亞歷山大」車身，但往後的二十輛則使用「Metsec」的產品。而原本裝在LV138上的『利蘭』GB350波箱亦於九三年被「勝利二型」的標準裝置「福伊特」DIWA851傳動系統取代。

「勝利二型」在八十年代已家傳戶曉，憑其特別高的重心，特大的馬力，為香港公共交通運輸歷史裡，多添十多宗翻車紀錄。中巴的LV21(CG5740)亦於九零年三月十日在鴨洲橋上翻側，也成為最後一輛翻車的「勝利二型」。

中巴於南區的路線在九三年失守，使原本屯駐於南區的「勝利二型」，被迫流徙到東區作為根據地。而「勝利二型」在當時的新任務是接收『佳牌』巴士的路線，跟著往日用『佳牌』巴士行走的14及47路線，在大潭道及域多利道上飛馳。

Description :

In one early morning in October 1979, a brand-new 'Victory Mk II' LV1 (CA9169) was seen climbing up the steep Battery Hill Road carrying over half a hundred passengers on route 41 heading towards Wa Fu Estate. The bus was equipped with a 'Gardner' 6LXB engine and gradually replaced the "Arab" buses.

The first 147 'Victory Mk II' buses of CMB used 'Alexander' bodies but the later 20 used 'Metsec' product. The "Leyland" GB350 gearbox originally used on LV138 was also replaced by the standard 'Voith' DIWA851 transmission on the 'Victory Mk IIs'.

'Victory Mk II' was a well-reported name in the 80s due to its extra-high centre of gravity, high horsepower output and caused over 10 overturned traffic accidents. The last one was on 19 March 1990 in which CMB's LV21 (CG5740) overturned on the Apleichau Bridge.

CMB lost its route for Island South and all 'Victory Mk IIs' for the South District were transferred to the Eastern District. Later on, the 'Victory Mk IIs' then took over the "Guy" 'Arab' buses on route 14 & 47 on Tai Tam Road and Victoria Road.
