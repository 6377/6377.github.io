名稱：「丹尼士」統治者型(又名「多明尼達型」)
Name : Dennis Dominator

車隊編號：DD/SD
Fleet No: DD/SD

內容簡介：

當香港島上已有二百多輛的『丹拿』「珍寶型」巴士行走時，『丹尼士』於七九年初向中巴提供了一輛「統治者」長陣版本巴士作試驗，命名為DD1(BX2164)，主要是與「珍寶型」一起行走本土線20及21路線。不論長度、引擎、懸掛系統，兩者均是一樣的。但是，「統治者型」所配上的「福伊特」全自動傳動系統，似乎比較先進得多，再加上首次在港露面的「東蘭開夏」車身，就顯得份外特別。

過了三年後，中巴再引入六部「統治者型」巴士，名為SD，外貌與其大哥的尺碼完全相同，新車則用上「亞歷山大」車身，並只有九點六米的長度，再加上四點二米的高度，用以代替15線的短陣『丹拿』「珍寶」。SD1至SD3用葉片彈簧懸掛，SD4至SD6則使用氣墊懸掛。

後期DD1 的企位完全取消，成為中巴車隊中唯一沒有企位的雙層巴士。至於SD1至SD6，由於其短身的車身，所以成為了山頂線的首選，平日多見於山頂15號路線。

Description :

When Hong Kong had over 200 "Daimler" 'Fleetline' buses in service , "Dennis" introduced to Hong Kong in 1979 a 'Dominator' long-chasis bus to CMB for trial run and was named DD1 (BX2164). It was used alongside with 'Fleetline' on routes 20 & 21. It had the same length, engine and suspension, but the 'Dominator' was equipped with the 'Voith' full-automatic transmission, and was more advanced in design. With the brand-new 'East Lancs' body in Hong Kong, it took on a special appearance here.

After 3 years, CMB introduced another 6 'Dominators' and named them SDs with a completely new look. The new model uses 'Alexander' bodies with a length of 9.6 Metre; height was 4.2 Metre. They were used to replace the "Daimler" 'Fleetline' for route 15. And SD1 to SD3 used leaf spring suspension while SD4 to SD6 used air suspension.

Later on, DD1 had all its standing removed and was the only double-decker bus of CMB fleet without standing passengers. SD1 to SD6, on the other hand, were mostly used for the Peak route 15 because of their lower height.
