名稱：「利蘭」奧林比安型十一米(空調)
Name : Leyland Olympian 11 Metre (Air-conditioned)

車隊編號：LA
Fleet No: LA

內容簡介：

新世界第一巴士服務有限公司接手經營中巴的專利權後，向中巴購入大部份巴士，其中空調巴士為數一百九十九部，但全部都成為新巴經營初期的空調巴士主力，亦是新巴直接跟其它的對手爭取乘客的有力武器。

這一百九十九部空調巴士中，『利蘭』「奧林比安型」十一米LA巴士佔二十五輛，車身色彩在服後期陸續換上新巴色彩。

Description :

After New World First Bus sucessfully acquired the franchise from the government, a number of 199 air-conditioned buses were purchased from CMB, they were commissioned on 1 September 1998, all ex-CMB air-conditioned buses play a significant role in NWFB's fleet.

These 199 buses includes 25 "Leyland" 'Olympian' LA 11 Metrre air-conditioned double deck buses, later, they were repainted with NWFB's standard colour. The newly repainted "Leyland" 'Olympian' has white roof instead the orange colour on the "Dennis" 'Trident'.


帶著新巴色彩的『利蘭』「奧林比安型」LA10，綠色色調並沒有完全將車頂覆蓋，而留下較多的白色色彩。
The newly repainted "Leyland" 'Olympian' LA10, has white colour roof instead the orange colour on the "Dennis" 'Trident'.

新巴的LA8重新投入590路線，同時亦將新巴的新色彩帶到海怡半島、鴨洲新及港島南區一帶。
The air-con "Leyland" 'Olympian' LA8 returns to serve on route 590 and also brings the new NWFB livery to Island South.

綠色、白色及橙色是構成新巴空調巴士標準色彩的主色調。唯一跟低地台空調車的分別是車身上沒有波浪紋。
The NWFB livery on the ex-CMB air-con double decker buses is formed by green, white and orange colours without stream-line.

新巴最先將『利蘭』「奧林比安型」LA雙層空調巴士加以翻髹，確保能夠儘快建立新巴空調巴士的全新形象。
NWFB "Leyland " 'Olympian' LA air-conditioned double deck buses were the first to repaint in NWFB's fleet. This is to ensure to establish the newly NWFB air-conditioned buses image.
