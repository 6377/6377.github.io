名稱：「利蘭」勝利二型
Name : Leyland Victory II

內容簡介：

『利蘭』「勝利二型」在城巴車隊出現，是源自於一九九三年九月，城巴手經營廿六條專利巴士線開始。當時有個別路線，如76號路線，須攀爬陡斜的山路，而城巴亦無意派全新空調車行走，另外，來自新加坡的『利蘭』「亞特蘭大型」又缺乏這方面的能耐。因此，城巴只有與九巴、中巴等看齊，亦都選用這款前置引擎及爬山性能不弱的「勝利二型」。

這些『利蘭』「勝利二型」當然不是全新巴士，而是城巴向大嶼山巴士公司購入的。至九六年四月底，仍被派出服務乘客的城巴「勝利二型」，只下三輛，車隊編號分別是37、40及45。

Description :

"Leyland" 'Victory II' entered the CTB fleet in September 1993 in advance of the introduction of Network 26. Ten buses, 36-45 were purchased from NLB, bringing with them a surprisingly diverse range of body style for such a small batch of vehicles. 36 & 38 were new to NLB and assembled in Singapore by Chua Heng, featuring CMB style/route number screens and reaward sliding cab doors. Also new to NLB, 37, was constructed by KMB and featured a CMB style destination screen and cab door, and unusually for a late model the early style shallow windscreen. 38 was constructed by KMB to the then current KMB specification, with deep windscreen, one piece front destination equipment and forward sliding cab door. 40-45 all initially operated with KMB and were of similar style to 38. By May 1996, only 37, 40 and 45 remained in passenger service, 36 had been converted to an open top for tree lopping duties, while 38 and 44 are under conversion to service tenders.

城巴車隊編號37的『利蘭』「勝利二型」行走5號路線，以支援其他『利蘭』「亞特蘭大」巴士的不足。
CTB's 37 "Leyland" 'Victory MKII' was served route 5 in supporting "Leyland" 'Atlantean'.

編號45是城巴車隊中最後一輛『利蘭』「勝利二型」，在海洋公園總內留影。
CTB's 45 was the last "Leyland" 'Victory MKII' seen in the Ocean Park Bus Terminus.

編號40是城巴車隊中，僅存少量的『利蘭』「勝利二型」巴士，仍保留第一手在九巴時的淡綠色彩。
No.40 was one of the few remaining "Leyland" 'Victory MKII' buses in CTB bus fleet.
