KMB PERFORMANCE PLEDGE REPORT

1995-96



To show our firm commitment to the provision of a quality and reliable bus service, KMB made a Performance Pledge in May 1993 and set targets on mechanical reliability and operation capability, the two core competencies in bus operation. We are pleased to announce our 1995-96 performance against these targets for the year ended 30 June 1996 as follows :

We are pleased to be able to report continued improvements in the mechanical reliability of our buses and also in our capacity to meet the peak schedule to provide adequate bus services to our customers. The 1995-96 performance is better than the pledged target on both counts and since the pledge was first made in 1993, sustained improvements have been achieved. With the implementation of a quality management system in our vehicle maintenance activities and ISO 9002 accreditation of our bus depots, we envisage that we will be able to continue to maintain a record of improvement in mechanical reliability in the coming year. In the area of operation capability, we have deployed our resources to the limit to achieve the substantial improvement recorded last year. We are less optimistic that a significant increase will be registered in the near future since our ability to operate departures in the peak period is constrained by external factors such as road congestion, weather fluctuation, etc. Nonetheless, we are encouraged that Government has initiated an inter-district bus-only lane study aimed at improving the operating environment of buses. We hope that the implementation of bus priority measures arising from the study will assist us in providing a better bus service for Hong Kong.

The annual report on KMB's Performance Pledge 1996-97 will be published in the third quarter of 1997 to cover the year ending 30 June 1997.

RESULT

The unaudited profit attributable to Shareholders for the six months ended 30 June 1996 amounted to HK$241.5 million (1995 - HK$218 million). As the profit from franchised bus operations for the period under review fell short of the permitted return by HK$9.5 million, the full amount retained in the Development Fund of HK$2 million is notionally transferred to the profit and loss account. The actual amount to be transferred under the Profit Control Scheme will be determined at the end of the year when the full year's results are known.

INTERIM DIVIDEND

The Board has declared an interim dividend of 24 cents per share (1995 - 22 cents per share), totalling HK$96.9 million (1995 - HK$88.8 million), for the year ending 31 December 1996. This interim dividend will be paid on 10 October 1996 to the Shareholders who are on the Register of Members at the close of business on 4 October 1996. The Register will be closed from 2 October 1996 to 4 October 1996, both dates inclusive. To qualify for the interim dividend, all transfer forms accompanied by the relevant share certificates must be lodged with the Company's Registrars, Central Registration Hong Kong Limited of 17/Fl., Hopewell Centre, 183 Queen's Road East, Hong Kong not later than 4:00 p.m. on 1 October 1996.

REVIEW OF OPERATIONS

The total ridership for the first six months of 1996 was 506.7 million passenger trips, or a daily average of 2.78 million, representing an increase of 1.8% when compared to the same period last year. Revenue and operating costs for the first six months of 1996 increased by approximately 11.2% and 11.5% respectively as compared with the same period last year. The increase in revenue was mainly due to the fare increases (averaging 10% for cross-harbour routes from 3 March 1996 and 2.7% for other routes from 4 April 1996) and the increase in ridership. The increase in operating costs was due to the increases in salaries and wages and other operating costs.

NEW BUSES

During the period under review, 140 new air-conditioned double deck buses and 3 new air-conditioned single deck buses were licensed. As at 30 June 1996, 164 chassis were under various stages of body assembly.

NEW ROUTES

In the first half of 1996, five new routes were introduced to cater for the high morning peak demand.

WAGES INCREASE

With effect from 1 June 1996, about 9,700 daily-rated employees were granted an average pay rise of 7.71%. This pay increase represented an additional annual cost to the Company of some HK$131.8 million. As part of the pay rise, both the good service bonus and the safe driving bonus for bus captains operating double deck buses and medium air-conditioned single deck buses have increased by HK$3.00 to HK$53.50 per day and by HK$2.50 to HK$45.0 per day respectively. The average monthly take-home pay of bus captains operating double deck buses after the pay rise has risen to about HK$13,710; for those operating the 12-metre and 11-metre double deck buses, it has increased to about HK$15,010. The average pay for a new bus captain operating double deck buses will be about HK$12,730. The monthly take-home pay of new bus captains of medium air-conditioned single deck buses will be about HK$9,590. As at 30 June 1996, the Company had a total workforce of 11,786 employees.

DEALINGS IN THE COMPANY'S LISTED SECURITIES

During the period, the Company has not purchased, sold or redeemed any of the Company's own shares.

COMPLIANCE WITH THE CODE OF BEST PRACTICE

No director of the Company is aware of information which would reasonably indicate that the Company is not or was not in compliance with the Code of Best Practice, as set out by the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited in Appendix 14 to the Listing Rules at any time during the six months ended 30 June 1996.

PROSPECTS

KMB's current franchise will expire on 31 August 1997. Discussions with the Government for the extension of the Company's franchise are still in progress. We are confident that the Company will eventually be granted a new or extended franchise that would provide the Company with a sound basis upon which we can plan for the future.

During the period, Long Win Holdings Limited which is a wholly owned subsidiary of KMB submitted three tenders to operate bus routes to the new Chek Lap Kok Airport and to North Lantau which, if successful, will provide considerable scope for the expansion of the Company's services. It is expected that the tender results will be announced in September.

We will continue to focus on attracting more passengers by improving the quality of bus services to meet the rising expectations of the community. We will also seek to improve the Company's productivity through further rationalising our route network and enhancing the efficiency of our Company's operating systems. Your directors have confidence that the Company will be able to continue its growth in the future.


P. C. Woo
Chairman
