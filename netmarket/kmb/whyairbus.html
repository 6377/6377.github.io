"Airbus"
" - Your right choice of transport to/from the airport"
The fast air-conditioned 'Airbus' connects the airport with most major hotels in Hong Kong. Take the 'Airbus', you can forget about uncertain taxi fares and double tunnel charges.

ANNOUNCEMENT ABOUT STOPS

English and Mandarin announcements on the buses tell you where to alight for your hotel.
FREE 'AIRBUS' ROUTE MAPS & TOURIST INFORMATION

'Airbus' route maps indicating location of major hotels or landmarks along the Airbus routes are available on buses for passengers.
FREQUENT SERVICE

Departure every 10-15 minutes From 7:00 a.m. to 12:00 midnight , except A5 & A7
(Route No.A5, From 7:00a.m. to 11:00p.m.)
(Route No.A7, From 8:00a.m. to 11:00p.m.)

FREE LUGGAGE STORAGE

Up to two pieces of luggage per passenger, each at maximum size 0.1m(3), are allowed to be stored in the luggage compartment on the buses.
VALUE FOR MONEY

Full fares for the 'Airbus' are unbeatable :
Route No.A1HK$12.00
Routes Nos.A2,A3,A5HK$17.00
Route No.A7HK$6.50
(Half fare for children under 12)
