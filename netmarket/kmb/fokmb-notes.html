1. Do not board or alight while bus is in motion.
  2. Passengers should board or alight at specified doors.
  3. Do not obstruct the staircase or doorways.
  4. No standing on upper deck or staircase.
  5. Do not put any part of the body out through the windows.
  6. Do not eat or drink inside the bus. Keep bus compartment clean.
  7. All upper deck windows must remain opened during typhoons. (Except air-conditioned double-deck buses)
  8. Smoking is prohibited on buses.
  9. Only parcels of a volume less than 0.1 cubic metre and which are not dangerous would be allowed on buses.
  10. Do not bring any animal onto the bus.
  11. Tender exact fare. Passengers are not allowed to collect bus fare from other passengers inside bus compartment.
  12. Children below the age of twelve are charged half fare to the nearest ten cents above.
  13. Children below the age of four, if accompanied by a fare-paying passenger and not occupying a seat can travel free of charge. The maximum number of children travelling free per adult is two.
  14. The scheduled bus frequency may be adjusted subject to traffic condition without prior notice to passengers.
  15. Passengers should not board the bus if the bus captain advises that it is full to its licensed capacity.
