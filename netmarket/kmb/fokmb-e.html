In the spirit of creating a more pleasant travelling environment for passengers, KMB would like to invite you to come and join FRIENDS OF KMB. With the aim to enhance passengers' travelling environment by establishing a set of simple practices for members to observe, FRIENDS OF KMB is designed to be both beneficial to the community and rewarding to its members.

Come along and pitch in for a good cause and enjoy a variety of privileges!


As a FRIEND OF KMB, you will enjoy:
  • Being closely related with KMB and helping it improve its services for the benefit of the community
  • Many opportunities to help and serve others whenever you travel with KMB
  • Having better knowledge of the operation and management structure of KMB to obtain more enjoyable rides with KMB
  • The opportunity to be chosen as a SPECIAL DUTY MEMBER with a complimentary KMB bus pass to help strengthen KMB's services to other passengers on a voluntary basis

  • Member Identification Card and Badge
  • Attractive FRIENDS OF KMB T-Shirt
  • Fantastic Buying Discounts
  • Exclusive Tours to KMB Depots
  • Preferred KMB Souvenirs
  • Various other activities including Annual Fun-Fairs and Buffets
  • Complimentary KMB Bus Pass for SPECIAL DUTY MEMBERS
Just spring into action in accordance with FRIENDS OF KMB Codes and watch your privileges unfold!
