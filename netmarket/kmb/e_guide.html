THE KMB PASSENGER ENQUIRY HOTLINE

24-HOUR FULLY AUTOMATED SERVICE

Through the Hotline's fully automated system, you can listen to pre-recorded or obtain fax-back bus route information 24 hours a day using a touchtone telephone.

OPERATOR SERVICE DURING OFFICE HOURS

You can speak directly to a Hotline Operator to express your views on KMB services or to seek other enquiry assistance during office hours.

Please use a touchtone telephone to call 2745-4466. Then follow the voice instruction to obtain the desired route information or services. Thank you.
