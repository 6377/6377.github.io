巴 士 迷 世 界 簡 介 INTRODUCTION OF BUS FAN WORLD


       "Bus Fan World" is a registered non-profit-making organization
established in  May  of 1992.  It aims at providing paths for bus fans,  
to  tie each other together and to promote friendship of bus fans.

      In  order to fullfill the needs of members,  "Bus Fan World" provides
various activities for members, We have had more than 800 bus fans joining 
"Bus Fan World" in May, 1996.

        "Bus fan World" organizes various bus-related activities regularly, such
as  bus trips, bus talks, quizes, bus-depots visits and lunch/dinner gathering.
These provide chances for  members  to widen their horizons.

"BUS FOCUS" is published by "Bus Fan World", which contains latest route news,
public transport news and fleet news of bus companies.  Besides, there are also
articles about bus routes history, bus gossips, bus fans' special interests,
and information about world-wide buses, to let members knows more about 
buses.
Electronic copy of BUS FOCUS is avaliable to public at the homepage of Bus Fan
World, which launched at July 1996.  The URL of Bus Fan World Homepage is:
"http://www.hkabc.net/~bfw".

Bus Fan World is managed by the "executive committee", which branches to 
"executive board", "editorial board" and "secretarial board".  They are 
responsible to administractive affairs, publication of BUS FOCUS and
managing members' affairs respectively.

〝巴士迷世界〞是一個非牟利的註冊團體，在一九九二年五月正式成立。目的是為巴士愛好
者提供途徑，增進彼止之間的團結及友誼。


〝巴士迷世界〞為會員提供各式各樣不同的活動，滿足各會員需要，直到一九九六年五月，
巴士迷世界已擁有超過八百名會員。


〝巴士迷世界〞時常舉辦各類型與巴士有關的活動，例如巴士遊河、巴士講座、問答遊戲、
參觀車廠及會員聚餐等，讓各會員交流彼此的興趣，增廣見聞。


「巴士之訊」是巴士迷世界出版的刊物。「巴士之訊」提供最新的巴士路線、公共交通新聞
及巴士公司車隊轉變等資料。

除此之外，「巴士之訊」亦有文章介紹巴士路線歷史、巴士趣聞，巴士迷特有興趣、及世界
其他地方的巴士。增進會員對巴士的認識。電子版的「巴士之訊」已放在〝巴士迷世界〞的
網頁上，其網頁地址為"http://www.hkabc.net/~bfw"。〝巴士迷世界網頁〞於九六年
七月正式為各界服務。

〝巴士迷世界〞由「行政委員會」去管理。行政委員會屬下有“行政組”、“秘書處”及“
編輯部”三個小組，分別負責行政、會員事務及出版「巴士之訊」。
